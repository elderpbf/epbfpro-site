Courses & Trilha

I didn't receive the access code.

Check your spam folder. The code expires within a few minutes; if it's slow, wait about a minute and tap “Resend” on the access screen.

The code doesn't work or my trail link expired.

Access codes and links have an expiry. Ask your instructor for a fresh link, or reach us.

I registered and I'm awaiting approval, or my access was blocked.

Some classes approve access manually. Your instructor will release it shortly; if you think it's a mistake, reach out.

How do I save the trail as an app on my phone?

On the trail screen, use “Save as app” (or “Add to Home Screen”) to install the shortcut on your phone.

Certificates

How do I validate my certificate?

Go to pensoia.com/trilha/validar and enter the code printed on the certificate.

I finished the course but didn't receive the certificate.

Certificates are issued after the course ends. If yours has finished and you still haven't received it, reach us.

Do my PDFs go to the cloud?

No. All extraction and OCR run on your own computer; no PDF or extracted text is sent to servers. Details in the Privacy Policy.

I didn't receive the login code.

Login is by e-mail, with a verification code. Check your spam folder. If it doesn't arrive, reach us.

Which systems does it run on, and how many devices?

It's a Windows application, used on up to 2 devices per account.
