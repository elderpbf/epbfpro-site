Where does teal live?

In the current scheme, teal jumps between two parts of the mark depending on the background. On bg.white the brain (bubble) is teal. On bg.navy the P outer is teal. Teal is the brand's "AI / intelligence" accent, and the part of the mark wearing it shifts. This study compares the current behavior against two alternatives that pin teal to a single semantic role.

Each row renders the mark across all four backgrounds (bg.white, bg.navy, bg.teal, bg.trasp) so the trade-off is visible at a glance.

ACurrent scheme, teal location inverts by background

On light surfaces the brain is teal and the P outer is navy. On dark surfaces the P outer is teal and the brain is white. The teal accent "swaps" places. This is the shipping behavior of stdColors() in brand-logos.js.

What this study reveals

Scheme A (current) is the least defensible

It doesn't pick a side. Teal is a brand asset; treating it as a "wherever-contrast-allows" token gives up the semantic anchor. A viewer of the mark can't say what teal "is" in the PensoIA brand — the brain on light, the P outline on dark — because there's no rule.

Scheme B is the strongest semantic match

The brain stroke is literally a brain (the AI idea). The "IA" letters are literally "AI". They share teal because they share meaning. The P, freed from the teal duty, takes on the name's color and reads as the first letter of "pensoIA". Mark + wordmark become one continuous word visually, with teal pulling attention to the AI accent (brain + IA). This is also the conventional pattern in tech logos with a separable mark + colored accent (Slack's old logo, several others).

Scheme C is a defensible alternative but weaker

It also pins teal to a single role, but the role is "the P is the AI". This is conceptually fine but it overloads the P with both "we are pensoIA" and "we are AI". The brain stroke becomes pure ornament once teal is removed from it. The "P is AI, but also the brain isn't AI anymore" message is murky.

Recommendation

Switch to Scheme B. It keeps the teal semantic clean (brain + IA share it), removes the inversion between light and dark, and makes the mark + wordmark read as one continuous lockup. Update stdColors() in brand-logos.js to emit Scheme B colors, then re-render the showcase.

Caveat on bg.teal

Both B and C need a fallback for bg.teal because the accent color is the surface color. Since bg.teal is currently cut from the canonical set (no real consumer for it), this is academic, but the fallback is easy if it ever comes back: shift the teal accent to navy on teal surfaces.
