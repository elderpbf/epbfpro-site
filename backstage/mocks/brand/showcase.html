Plain-text fallback, contexts without images

In fields with no image support (plaintext email, code, terminal, some form inputs), the mark is the name spelled out in whatever font is available. There's no "invisible logo", it's plain text. Brand recognition happens through consistent typography wherever Comfortaa loads, and through the name written correctly everywhere else.

What didn't make the canonical set

Every shipping variant needs a real consumer. These were cut because the cost of maintaining, rendering and validating them didn't pay rent:

  • stack, very rare use. Bring back when a real consumer shows up (specific square crop, vertical sidebar).
  • tag.duo, second color on the tagline added complexity without consistent payoff.
  • smallp-wordmark, in image-less contexts we use plain text (previous section), not another letter-as-image.
  • wordmark alone, text without the P loses the visual identity; prefer the full lockup.
  • bg.teal, no real teal surfaces where the mark lives today.

Everything cut lives in archive/, accessible if a real need reappears.
